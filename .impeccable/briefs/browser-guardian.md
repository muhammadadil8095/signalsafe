# Surface: SignalSafe Browser Guardian popup

Mode: Operate
Audience: campus students and faculty
Job: authenticate, scan the current tab URL, read the institutional verdict, act on high risk

## Direction contract

THESIS: A night-lab transilluminator, not a security dashboard. Risk is whether the sample fluoresces; the category-default metric-card stack is refused.

OWN-WORLD: Deep charcoal cabinet (#0c1218) with a navy well (#121c2c). Electric green (#3dff9a) is fluorescence, used only for safe/live signal and the primary action. Cyan (#5ee9ff) marks the sample identity (URL/domain). Amber and crimson are reserved for suspicious and malicious fluorescence, never decorative. Hairline well edges, flat controls, Red Hat Text for UI, Red Hat Mono for scores and timestamps.

STORY: You already opened a page. The Guardian tells you what SignalSafe Campus already knows about that URL, then offers a way back if the sample lights up as danger.

FIRST VIEWPORT: 380×560 popup. Top: committed navy identity bar with mark + “Browser Guardian” + session chip. Below: the current URL as a single sample strip. Primary action sits under the strip (Scan Current Website / Sign in). After a scan, a horizontal fluorescence band fills by risk_score; three compact lanes (ML, VirusTotal, Heuristic) sit under it; recommendations are a short list, not cards. Signature interaction: the band fills once, like a gel lighting under UV, then holds.

FORM: Assigned UV gel tray (candidate 5). seed:337720590229551f. Raises: junction-only copy (one status line per state); scan modules as labeled pass segments; flap-tick of the status verb when a scan completes.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
